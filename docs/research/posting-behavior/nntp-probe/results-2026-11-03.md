Conclusions:
- NEWNEWS is off on every provider, so NEWNEWS bisection is a dead end.
- No provider returns NNTP-Posting-Date, Injection-Info, Path or X-Received-Body-CRC through XHDR/HDR. Those cost one HEAD per article. Atlas's clock must come from Message-IDs (Nyuu ms, server-ID decodes) and article-number rank.
- Lines is empty or 0 on a large share of rows everywhere except Giganews and newsgroupdirect: always use :bytes.
- The Date parser must accept 2-digit years on every provider (13-68% of rows).
- Giganews (super.newsgroupdirect) runs 5.7 s ahead: correct per-provider skew before any time join.
- Compressed overview: XZVER works on XSNews and Usenet.Farm. XFEATURE COMPRESS GZIP works on Highwinds, Giganews, newsgroupdirect and frugal. Highwinds XZVER hangs, so use GZIP there.
- Server-assigned ID shapes were not seen: alt.binaries.test is mostly Nyuu client IDs. A re-run on a busy obfuscated binaries group would sample them.
- Config: newshosting appears twice; bonus.frugalusenet.com cannot select groups.
