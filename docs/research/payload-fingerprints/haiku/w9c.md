## Takeaways

- Best coverage per vendored byte: rarfile `test/files/` (106 files, covers RAR3 and RAR5 multi-volume, hp, psw, QO, solid, links, unicode, each with a `.exp` oracle). Confirm its license before vendoring.
- par2cmdline `tests/*.tar.gz` gives a set of small, targeted PAR2 sets (hash mismatch, 16k hash, subdirectories, codepage names). These are GPL, so check whether your Rust crate can include them or should fetch them at test time.
- libarchive `.uu` files are the cheapest RAR fixtures (uuencoded text), but the RAR-specific names were not confirmed in the upstream tree. Verify before relying on them.
- License cautions: par2cmdline (GPL), sabctools/sabyenc (GPL-family, UNVERIFIED), py7zr (LGPL, UNVERIFIED). Prefer generating fixtures with your own tools for yEnc and PAR2 tests, or fetching them at test time, rather than vendoring them.
- NZB: sanderjo/NZBs has no stated license. Use it only as an external download in tests, or write your own NZB fixtures, rather than committing its files.
