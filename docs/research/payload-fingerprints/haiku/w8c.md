## Key takeaways

- Use crc32fast 1.5.4 for CRC32. It is the most-used option in this set (821M downloads, 342 stars), and its `Hasher::combine` covers the zlib-style combine need. Verify the stored-CRC constructor before relying on it.
- Use the RustCrypto md-5, sha1 and sha2 crates for digests. They are pure Rust and have huge download counts. Skip ring because it is not pure Rust.
- Use xxhash-rust or twox-hash for xxHash. Both pass the bar. Write our own murmur3 (about 150 LOC) because no mmh3 crate meets the bar. Its only well-used candidates are stale (last release 2015 or 2021).
- Write our own for PAR2 (packets and GF(2^16) recovery, roughly 650 to 900 LOC), RAR4/RAR5 headers (about 600 LOC), 7z headers (about 600 LOC), yEnc (about 150 LOC), oshash and imohash, Bloom, MinHash and SimHash. The existing crates either have few stars, are GPL-tainted (rarpar), wrap C (unrar), are not PAR2-compatible (reed-solomon-simd), or are unmaintained.
- Open items I could not verify: stars for oshash, imohash, minhash-rs and simhash, and whether sevenz-rust2 has a header-only API. docs.rs and most GitHub API calls were blocked in this session, and the WebFetch budget was spent.
